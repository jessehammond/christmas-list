Fine print

By reading this list, you acknowledge that Jesse feels mildly icky about making one. For Jesse, Christmas is about memories and time spent together, not the gifts.

That said, Jesse knows there's real joy in giving, so this list sticks to things that have already come up in conversation and hobbies Jesse actually does. These are items Jesse isn't likely to buy for themselves. Jesse has thought about asking for no gifts, or for donations instead, but that takes away the fun of watching someone open something you picked out for them.

Then again, does a pre-approved list really do that either? Unclear. Jesse has spent years muddling over this without landing on a clear answer, and dreads being asked for a list of wants and desires every year. Something about it just doesn't feel right. So this year, Jesse is giving in and going with it.
